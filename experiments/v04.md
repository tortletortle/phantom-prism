# V0.4 synthetic inspection experiment

Scope: supervised synthetic tile inspection, fixed cell boxes; not YOLO, general object detection, physical imagery, or an adversarial benchmark.

Environment: Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.3.5, SciPy 1.17.0, scikit-learn 1.8.0. Run: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python inspection_demo.py`. Training seed 100; 1800 tiles. Test seeds 201, 202, 203; 60 images per seed, 9 cells per image. Fixed confidence threshold 0.5. Additive Gaussian noise std 0.12 in [0,1] pixel scale. Median filter fixed 3x3, applied independently per tile. No parameter search.

Observed AP50 by seed and pooled counts (TP/FP/FN at threshold):

| Condition | 201 | 202 | 203 | Mean AP50 | TP/FP/FN |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Clean | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 780/0/0 |
| Noise | .997771 | .997071 | .999496 | .998112 | 771/12/9 |
| Clean filtered | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 780/0/0 |
| Noise filtered | .995164 | .996249 | .998263 | .996559 | 753/10/27 |

Repeated execution in the same environment produced identical JSON bytes. All 19 prior unit tests passed (0.315 s). The new demo ran end-to-end; it has no dedicated new unit tests yet.

Interpretation: the fixed filter reduced false positives by 2 but increased false negatives by 18 on the noisy synthetic tiles; recall declined in all three test sets. This is not proof of statistical significance or generality. Single trained model; same synthetic generator, no real camera or physical print, fixed grid localization, no official COCOeval. No claim of novel defense.
